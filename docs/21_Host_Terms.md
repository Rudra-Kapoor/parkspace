## 1. Agreement and acceptance

1.1 This Agreement governs your use of the Platform as a Host. It applies in addition to
the Platform's general Terms of Service and to the Privacy Policy at
`24_Privacy_Policy.md`.

1.2 You accept this Agreement by creating a Host account, publishing a Space, or
accepting a Booking, whichever occurs first.

1.3 If you do not accept this Agreement, you must not list a Space.

1.4 We may amend this Agreement. We will give you notice of a material amendment by email
and in the Platform at least 14 days before it takes effect, except where a shorter period
is required by law or by a regulator. Continuing to list a Space after the effective date
constitutes acceptance. **REVIEW REQUIRED:** whether a unilateral amendment clause of this
form is enforceable against an individual Host in India, and whether a right to terminate
without penalty must be offered on notice of amendment.

1.5 Where this Agreement conflicts with the operational rules published in the Platform,
this Agreement prevails, except that the four cancellation policies and the fee
percentages are those recorded in `00_SPEC_KERNEL.md` sections 7 and 8 as implemented in
`platform_settings`.

2.7 We may refuse or close a Host account where you have previously been removed from the

the right to park in it.

Platform is permitted by, and does not breach:

(a) any title deed, conveyance, lease, licence or tenancy agreement affecting the Space;

(b) the bye-laws, rules or resolutions of any housing society, apartment owners
association, resident welfare association or management company having authority over
the Space;

(c) any mortgage, charge or other encumbrance on the property;

(d) any sanctioned building plan, occupancy certificate, completion certificate, land use
classification or municipal permission applying to the property;

(e) any condition attached to a commercial establishment's parking provision, including
any requirement that the parking be reserved for that establishment's own patrons, staff
or visitors; and

(f) any applicable law, rule or regulation.

3.3 Where the Space is inside a housing society, gated complex or managed building, you
warrant that you hold the permission of the society or management, and you will provide
evidence of that permission on request.

3.4 Where you are a tenant or licensee rather than an owner, you warrant that your

3.5 You warrant that the Space is not, and does not include any part of, a public road, a
footpath, a municipally controlled parking bay, a fire access route, a shared access way

3.6 You will notify us immediately, and withdraw the Space, if any warranty in this clause
3 ceases to be true, including if a society, landlord, employer or authority objects.

3.7 You acknowledge that we rely on the warranties in this clause 3. We do not
independently verify title, society permission, planning status or occupancy conditions,
and our verification process is a fraud control and a trust signal, not a legal opinion on
your right to let the Space.

3.8 **REVIEW REQUIRED:** whether the warranty and the indemnity at clause 16 are

## 4. Listing accuracy

4.1 Your listing must accurately describe the Space, including its location, its
dimensions, the vehicle sizes it can accommodate, the number of bays, the surface, the
level, whether it is covered, whether it is gated, and the access method.

4.2 Photographs must be of the actual Space, must be current, and must be taken by you or
used with permission. You must not use stock images or images of another space.

4.3 You must state the access instructions accurately and keep them current. If a gate
code changes, you must update it before the next Booking starts.

4.4 You must disclose any restriction that would matter to a Driver, including height
limits, width limits, tandem or blocked-in arrangements, time windows during which access
is not possible, security arrangements, and whether the Space is shared with others.

4.5 You must not list a Space you cannot actually make available, and you must not list
the same physical Space more than once or under more than one account.

4.6 The Platform shows only an approximate location, offset by 80 to 150 metres, plus the
street and locality, until a Booking is confirmed. Your exact address, gate number and
access instructions are released to a Driver only when that Driver holds a confirmed
Booking and only from 24 hours before the Booking starts. This is described in
`24_Privacy_Policy.md` and enforced technically, not merely as a policy.

4.7 You consent to that release. You should not list a Space if you are not willing for a
Driver with a confirmed Booking to receive your exact address and access instructions.

4.8 We may edit, reclassify, unpublish or remove a listing that is inaccurate, misleading,
unlawful, unsafe, or in breach of this Agreement.

## 5. Availability

5.1 You control your availability calendar, your price and your cancellation policy,
selected from the four policies in clause 9.

5.2 You must keep your availability accurate. A Space shown as available may be booked,
and a confirmed Booking is a firm commitment by you.

5.3 A Booking of a Space with more than one bay allocates one specific bay. You must make
that bay, or an equivalent bay at the same Space, available for the whole booked period.

5.4 If you use a channel or calendar outside the Platform, you are responsible for
preventing a clash. The Platform's exclusion constraint prevents two Platform Bookings
overlapping on the same bay. It cannot prevent a clash with an arrangement you made
elsewhere.

5.5 You must not ask a Driver to leave before the end of the booked period, except where
clause 11 permits it or where there is a genuine emergency.

## 6. Pricing and commission

6.1 You set the base price for your Space. The Platform may display a suggested price. A
suggestion is not a requirement and you are free to set any price within the permitted
range.

6.2 The amount the Driver pays is calculated by the Platform as set out in
`00_SPEC_KERNEL.md` section 7. In summary: the Driver pays the taxable amount, plus a
Driver service fee, plus any applicable tax on that fee.

6.3 We charge you a **Host commission of 10 percent** (`HOST_COMMISSION_PCT` default
0.10) of the taxable amount of each Booking. Your payout is the taxable amount less that
commission.

6.4 We separately charge the Driver a **service fee of 5 percent**
(`DRIVER_SERVICE_FEE_PCT` default 0.05) of the taxable amount. That fee is ours and is
not part of your earnings.

6.5 We may change the commission percentage on not less than 30 days' notice. A change
does not affect Bookings already confirmed.

6.6 Worked example on a Rs 500 booking: the Driver is charged Rs 500 plus a service fee
of Rs 25 plus tax on that fee of Rs 4.50, a total of Rs 529.50. Our commission is Rs 50.
Your payout is Rs 450. Full worked examples for every cancellation outcome are in
`23_Refund_Policy.md`.

6.7 All amounts are in Indian rupees. Internally the Platform holds amounts as integer
paise to avoid rounding error.

6.8 You must not require a Driver to pay you anything outside the Platform for a Booking
made through the Platform, and you must not solicit a Driver to transact off-Platform.
Doing so is a material breach.

7.6 You are responsible for the accuracy of your payout details. We are not liable for a
payment sent to an account you gave us incorrectly.

7.7 We may set off any amount you owe us against any amount we owe you.

## 9. Cancellation by you, and the reliability penalty

9.1 You select one of four cancellation policies for each Space, and it is shown to the
Driver before booking:

(a) `flexible`: the Driver receives a full refund of the base amount if they cancel more
than 1 hour before the Booking starts;

(b) `moderate`: full refund more than 24 hours before the start, half after that;

(c) `strict`: half refund more than 48 hours before the start, nothing after that;

(d) `non_refundable`: no refund once the Booking is confirmed. Available only for monthly
and event inventory.

9.2 The policy you select governs cancellation **by the Driver**. It does not limit your
obligations when **you** cancel.

9.3 If you cancel a confirmed Booking, the Driver receives a **full refund of everything
they paid**, including the Driver service fee and any tax on it, regardless of which
policy applies to the Space. You receive nothing for that Booking.

9.4 A Host cancellation also applies a reliability penalty. The penalty may include:

(a) a recorded reliability score reduction visible in your Host dashboard;

(b) suppression of the Space in search ranking for a period;

(c) automatic blocking of the cancelled dates so that the Space cannot be relet at a
higher price;

(d) a cancellation fee charged to you, set in `platform_settings` and disclosed before you
confirm the cancellation; and

(e) for repeated cancellations, suspension or removal of the Space or the account.

9.5 **REVIEW REQUIRED:** whether a cancellation fee charged to a Host is enforceable as
drafted, or whether it risks being characterised as a penalty rather than a genuine
pre-estimate of loss. Counsel must confirm the amount and the mechanism before this clause
is published.

9.6 The penalty does not apply where you cancel because of a genuine emergency or because
of circumstances beyond your reasonable control, evidenced to our reasonable satisfaction,
or where we agree the cancellation was caused by a Driver's breach.

9.7 You must cancel through the Platform. Telling a Driver directly not to come, without
cancelling in the Platform, is treated as a failure to provide the Space under clause 11
and is a more serious breach than a cancellation.

## 10. Driver conduct and damage

10.1 The Driver is responsible to you for their own conduct and for damage they cause to
the Space.

10.2 If a Driver damages the Space, you must report it through the Platform within 24
hours of the end of the Booking, with photographs and a description. Reporting late
materially weakens any claim.

10.3 We will provide the dispute process described in `23_Refund_Policy.md` section 8. We
may share relevant Booking records and evidence with both parties.

10.4 We are not the insurer of the Space. We do not guarantee that you will recover a
damage amount, and any recovery is a matter between you and the Driver. Where we hold
funds relating to the disputed Booking we may hold them pending the outcome.

10.5 You must not attempt to recover damages by immobilising, towing, damaging or
withholding a vehicle, or by preventing a Driver from leaving. Doing so may be unlawful
and is a material breach of this Agreement.

10.6 If a Driver overstays, the overstay charge in `22_Driver_Terms.md` clause 5 applies
after the grace period of 10 minutes (`GRACE_PERIOD_MINUTES` default 10). You must not
charge a separate overstay amount of your own.

10.7 You must treat Drivers respectfully and without discrimination on any ground
including religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth, language, disability or any other
protected characteristic.

## 11. Prohibited spaces and prohibited uses

11.1 You must not list:

(a) any part of a public road, footpath, or municipally controlled bay;

(b) a space you do not have the right to let, see clause 3;

(c) a space whose use for parking would breach a lease, society bye-law, planning
permission, occupancy certificate or licence;

(d) a space that is unsafe, structurally unsound, obstructed, or that requires a Driver to
pass through a hazardous area;

(e) a space that is a designated accessible bay, unless you are entitled to let it and it
is listed accurately as such;

(f) a space that blocks a fire route, an emergency access, a hydrant, or a statutory
access way;

(g) a space subject to a dispute over title or possession.

11.2 You must not:

(a) misrepresent the Space or its availability;

(b) create fake Bookings, or arrange Bookings from accounts you control or influence, to
inflate reviews, ratings, earnings or incentives;

(c) discriminate against Drivers on any protected ground;

(d) contact a Driver for any purpose other than the Booking, or retain or use a Driver's
personal data for any other purpose;

(e) install any camera or recording device that captures a Driver inside a vehicle, or
that captures an area beyond what is reasonably necessary for the security of your
property, without clear notice;

(f) solicit off-Platform payment, see clause 6.8;

(g) use the Platform for any unlawful purpose, or to store goods, run a business from the
Space, or allow any use other than parking a vehicle;

(h) take, keep or share a copy of a Driver's identity documents.

11.3 Breach of clause 11 may result in immediate removal of the Space, forfeiture of
pending payouts pending investigation, and termination.

## 12. Personal data

12.1 When a Driver books your Space, we share limited personal data with you so that the
Booking can happen: the Driver's display name, their vehicle details, the Booking times,
and a masked or in-Platform contact channel.

12.2 You may use that data only for the purposes of that Booking, for resolving a dispute
about it, and for complying with law. You must not use it for marketing, must not add it
to any other list, must not share it, and must delete it when it is no longer needed for
those purposes.

12.3 You must comply with applicable data protection law in respect of any personal data
you receive.

12.4 **REVIEW REQUIRED:** whether a Host receiving this data is an independent data
fiduciary, a joint fiduciary, or a processor, and what contractual terms must therefore
appear here. See `19_Privacy_Requirements.md` section 7.

## 13. Our role

13.1 We provide a technology platform that allows Hosts and Drivers to find each other,
agree a Booking and settle payment. We are not a parking operator, a car park, a bailee, a
valet, a security service or an insurer.

13.2 The contract for the parking of a vehicle is between you and the Driver. We are not a
party to it.

13.3 We do not take custody, possession or control of any vehicle at any time.

13.4 We do not supervise, secure, patrol, inspect or maintain any Space.

13.5 **REVIEW REQUIRED:** whether this characterisation holds in law, and whether any
Platform feature contradicts it in substance. See `20_Legal_Requirements.md` section 3.

14.1 **We do not provide any insurance to you, to the Driver, or in respect of any vehicle
or any Space.** There is no Platform damage cover, no theft cover and no liability cover.

14.2 You are responsible for arranging any insurance you consider appropriate for the
Space and for your property.

14.3 You should confirm with your own insurer whether your existing property or

document naming the insurer, the limits and the exclusions, and this clause will be

read as a promise of cover, and you should not rely on one.

## 17. Suspension and termination

17.1 You may stop listing at any time. You may close your Host account once you have no
upcoming or active Bookings, no open disputes and no unsettled amounts.

17.2 You must honour every Booking that is already confirmed at the time you stop listing,
or cancel it under clause 9 with the consequences set out there.

17.3 We may suspend or remove a Space, or suspend or terminate your account, immediately
where: a warranty in clause 3 is or appears to be untrue; a third party with apparent
authority objects to the listing; we reasonably suspect fraud; there is a risk to the
safety of any person; you materially breach this Agreement; or we are required to do so by
law or by a regulator.

17.4 We may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 30 days' notice.

17.5 On suspension we will normally tell you the reason and give you an opportunity to
respond, unless doing so would prejudice an investigation, breach a legal obligation, or
create a risk to another person.

17.6 On termination: confirmed Bookings are cancelled and Drivers refunded in full,
payouts properly due to you are released subject to clause 7.3, and clauses 8, 10.4, 12,
13, 14, 15, 16, 18 and 19 survive.
